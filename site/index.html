Overview

Every project, every agent, one window.

A tab for each pinned or recently active project, each in its own colour. The Overview lines up the agents of all of them: their state, their last action, their progress, and an Answer button where you are needed.

Projects fold into groups in a panel (Ctrl P). A tab shakes, and can chime, when one of its agents waits for you.

A board that moves on its own.

Answer a waiting agent or open it straight from its card. The board pops out in a window of its own.

Tickets

Your sprint, one column away.

Link your ticket tracker to a project and its current sprint lands in a Tickets column of the Kanban. Turn a ticket into a task for an agent: the ticket follows it into the agent's header and into your messages.

  • Projects suggested from your git branches and commits
  • Quick filters by person, status, type, priority and epic
  • A ticket panel beside the conversation, with its description, comments and links
